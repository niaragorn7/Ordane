# Domain definition

Customer has one or more Opportunities.
Opportunity has one or more Quotes.
Quote has one or more Quote Items.
Each Quote Item references one Product and specifies a quantity.
Each Quote can be converted into an Order. A Quote has at most one active (non-Cancelled)
Order at a time.

Approval is included as a simulation in V1 to keep statuses consistent.

--------------------
## Entities
Customer, Opportunity, Quote, QuoteItem, Product, Order, OrderItem

Customer
  └── Opportunity
        └── Quote
              ├── QuoteItem ──→ Product
              └── Order
                    └── OrderItem ──→ Product

Customer and Opportunity are Salesforce-owned and exist only in Salesforce. Ordane
persists the remaining entities.

---------------------

## Architecture and Ownership

Salesforce is the business-facing application. Ordane (Go / PostgreSQL) is the system of
record for the operational order domain. Salesforce initiates every synchronization.
There is no direct Salesforce ↔ AWS integration in V1:

Salesforce → Ordane → AWS → Ordane → Salesforce

Salesforce is authoritative for: Customer, Opportunity, Quote (record and user-facing
status), the approval workflow, and user-facing quote/order management.

Ordane is authoritative for: Product, QuoteItem (pricing and availability), Order,
OrderItem, order lifecycle, and pricing/availability processing. The Product catalog is
exposed to Salesforce through the Ordane API. QuoteItems are authored in Salesforce while
the Quote is Draft and persisted in Ordane from the Approved sync onward.

Identifiers: Ordane uses its own UUIDv7 identifiers as domain identifiers. Salesforce
stores the corresponding Ordane IDs for synchronization. Ordane does not persist Customer
or Opportunity. Its Quote record carries the Salesforce references
(`salesforce_quote_id`, `salesforce_opportunity_id`, `salesforce_customer_id`) as external
references only.

Ordane's Quote record and its QuoteItems are created when Salesforce syncs an Approved
Quote. The sync is an idempotent upsert keyed on `salesforce_quote_id`, and each re-sync
replaces the Ordane-side QuoteItems for that Quote. Quote.status in Ordane is a mirror of
the Salesforce status; Salesforce remains authoritative. The Quote record exists so
PostgreSQL can enforce `Order.quote_id` as a foreign key.

## Pricing

For V1:
- No PriceList, PriceListItem, or complex pricing rules.
- A QuoteItem is created with a product and quantity only, without a price.
- AWS determines the actual price. Ordane stores the result and returns it to Salesforce.
- QuoteItem.unit_price is null until AWS responds, then represents the price agreed for
  that Quote.
- Quote.total_price = Σ(quantity × unit_price), populated once all items are priced.
- Product.price is an informational catalog price. It is not used to price or recalculate
  Quotes.
- Pricing becomes a proper domain in V2.

Pricing flow:

Salesforce (Quote + Quote Items: product, quantity)
   ↓  Approved
Ordane
   ↓
AWS (pricing / availability)
   ↓
Ordane (stores result)
   ↓
Salesforce (applies Accepted / Rejected, receives pricing)

## Relationships
+ Quote 1 ── N QuoteItem
+ Product 1 ── N QuoteItem
+ Quote 1 ── N Order (at most one active, non-Cancelled Order at a time)
+ Order 1 ── N OrderItem
+ Product 1 ── N OrderItem

Customer 1 ── N Opportunity 1 ── N Quote holds in Salesforce only.

## Statuses

Quote (status owned by Salesforce)
- Draft: not yet submitted. Fully editable.
- Pending Approval: submitted, awaiting the simulated approval. Salesforce-side only, with
  no synchronization with Ordane. Not editable; can be cancelled with a button.
- Approved: approved, awaiting Ordane/AWS pricing and availability. Not editable.
  Synchronization with Ordane begins here.
- Accepted: AWS confirmed availability and returned pricing. Not editable.
- Rejected: rejected by the approval or by the availability check. Always carries a
  rejection_reason identifying the source. Returns to Draft to be edited and resubmitted.

Order (status owned by Ordane)
- Draft: created from an Accepted Quote, not yet submitted.
- Submitted: sent to AWS.
- Confirmed: AWS responded successfully.
- Completed: fulfilled. Terminal.
- Failed: AWS failure.
- Cancelled: cancelled before completion. Terminal.

## O2C Rules

### Quote flow
1. A Quote is created in Salesforce as Draft. Items (product + quantity, no price) are
   added and remain fully editable. Nothing is synchronized with Ordane at this stage.
2. Submit (Draft → Pending Approval) requires at least one QuoteItem. A pending Quote can
   be cancelled (Pending Approval → Draft).
3. The simulated approval resolves to Approved or Rejected. Both happen entirely within
   Salesforce.
4. Rejected: the Quote returns to Draft, is edited according to the rejection_reason, and
   is resubmitted, re-running approval.
5. Approved: Salesforce sends the Quote data to Ordane. Ordane creates or updates its
   Quote record and QuoteItems (idempotent upsert on `salesforce_quote_id`).
6. Ordane requests pricing and availability from AWS, stores the result, and returns it
   to Salesforce.
7. If available, unit prices and total_price are populated and Salesforce applies
   Accepted. If not, Salesforce applies Rejected with the AWS reason in rejection_reason.

### Order flow
1. An Order can only be created from an Accepted Quote, initiated from within the Quote in
   Salesforce. Ordane rejects Order creation unless its mirrored Quote status is
   Accepted. The Order starts as Draft, with OrderItems copied from the QuoteItems
   (product, quantity, unit_price) as an operational snapshot.
2. Order quantities must equal Quote quantities (no partial orders in V1).
3. Submit (Draft → Submitted) is an explicit user action. It requires at least one
   OrderItem and the required Order fields to be present.
4. AWS returns 201 Created → Confirmed. AWS failure → Failed. Failed → Submitted is a
   retry.
5. Confirmed → Completed on fulfilment. Draft, Submitted, Failed and Confirmed Orders can
   be Cancelled. Completed and Cancelled are terminal.

### Quote changes after Order generation
- Order in Draft: update or regenerate it from the Quote.
- Order in Submitted or Confirmed: the revision process cancels the active Order. The
  revised Quote goes through approval again, then through the Ordane/AWS availability and
  pricing check again, and a new Order is generated from it.
- Revising an Accepted Quote is Accepted → Draft. It is not allowed if the Quote's Order
  is Completed.
- The Quote is the commercial source of truth; the Order is the operational snapshot.

### Status Transitions
Quote
  Draft → Pending Approval
  Pending Approval → Draft (cancelled)
  Pending Approval → Approved
  Pending Approval → Rejected
  Approved → Accepted
  Approved → Rejected
  Rejected → Draft
  Accepted → Draft (revision)

Order
  Draft → Submitted
  Draft → Cancelled
  Submitted → Confirmed
  Submitted → Failed
  Submitted → Cancelled
  Failed → Submitted
  Failed → Cancelled
  Confirmed → Completed
  Confirmed → Cancelled

## Core Invariants

- Salesforce enforces that an Opportunity belongs to a Customer and a Quote to an
  Opportunity. Ordane requires salesforce_opportunity_id and salesforce_customer_id to be
  present on every Quote.
- A Quote must contain at least one QuoteItem at submission.
- A QuoteItem must reference exactly one Product.
- An Order can only be generated from an Accepted Quote.
- A Quote can have at most one active (non-Cancelled) Order.
- An Order must contain at least one OrderItem.
- An OrderItem must reference exactly one Product.
- Order quantities must equal Quote quantities for every product (V1, no partial orders).
- A Quote whose Order is Completed cannot be revised.
- Invalid status transitions must be rejected.
- Completed and Cancelled Orders cannot transition to another status.
- Salesforce IDs are external references only; Ordane UUIDv7 is the domain identifier.

--------------------

## Boundaries for Version 1 (V1)

V1 focuses exclusively on the core B2B quote-to-order flow.

### Included
- Customer and Opportunity management (Salesforce-side only)
- Product catalog
- Quote creation and management
- Quote Items, with pricing determined by AWS
- Quote approval simulation
- AWS pricing and inventory availability check
- Order generation from an Accepted Quote
- Order Items
- Order submission and status tracking
- Salesforce ↔ Ordane ↔ AWS integration

### Out of Scope
- Partial orders
- Multiple concurrent active Orders per Quote
- Revision of a Quote whose Order is Completed (deferred to a later version)
- Inventory management
- Manufacturing
- Logistics and fulfillment management
- Automated approval workflows
- Advanced pricing (PriceList, PriceListItem, pricing rules)
- Analytics and reporting
- Mobile application
- Machine learning / AI
